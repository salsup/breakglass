Break Glass privacy policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Break Glass has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us. We can't see your location, messages, plans or files.

What stays on your phone

  • Places and trail: waypoints, your breadcrumb trail and navigation targets.
  • Messages: Talk messages you send and receive. They are kept so they can be passed on to phones you meet later.
  • Family plan: people, medical needs, meeting places, contacts, checklists and notes. It leaves the phone only when you share it, for example by AirDrop or as a PDF.
  • Household: ages, needs and pets you enter, and your county. Your location is used once, on the phone, to look up your county in data stored in the app. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • My files: documents and page photos you add, and the searchable text Break Glass reads from them.
  • Medical ID: what you enter in Tools → Medical ID.
  • Supplies and outage clock: water, food, medicines and fuel you count, when the power went out, and battery readings for the estimate in Phone power.
  • Settings: things like night mode, language and your Talk name.

Talk: phone to phone

  • Who receives messages: messages go directly to nearby phones running Break Glass over Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth, and those phones pass them on to others. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Open channel: anyone nearby with Break Glass can read the open channel. Your Talk name, battery level and messages are visible there.
  • Private groups: messages are encrypted with your group's passphrase. Only people with the same passphrase can read them; other phones relay them without being able to read them.
  • Your location: an SOS always includes your position and goes to every nearby Break Glass phone. When "Share my location with my groups" is on (it is on unless you turn it off), your private groups see your position, and "I'm OK" check-ins, which also go to every nearby Break Glass phone, include it.
  • Your Talk identity: each phone makes a random key so others can recognize your messages. It isn't linked to your name, phone number or Apple Account.
  • No emergency services: Talk and SOS reach only other Break Glass phones. They don't contact 911 or any other emergency service.

Downloads

  • Offline library and maps: optional packs download from the Lastbook project's server (downloads.lastbook.org). Like any website, that server and its hosting provider receive your IP address and which files you ask for. Break Glass sends nothing else, and we don't receive that information.
  • Manuals and guides: optional manuals download straight from their publishers' websites: the US Army (armypubs.army.mil), the Defense Department (apps.dtic.mil, champ.usuhs.edu), USDA (research.fs.usda.gov, ars.usda.gov), FEMA (ready.gov), govinfo.gov, the European Medicines Agency (ema.europa.eu) and the National Center for Home Food Preservation (nchfp.uga.edu). Like any website, they receive your IP address and which file you ask for.
  • Translation: offline translation in Lens uses Apple's Translation framework. Apple may download language files when you ask, under Apple's privacy policy.

Ask

Ask runs entirely on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. Your questions and the answers aren't sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app (waypoints, trail, family plan, My files, downloaded packs), or delete Break Glass to remove everything it stored. Copies you sent to other people stay on their phones.

Children

Break Glass doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal data at all.
