Break Glass support

Questions, problems or a report about a Talk message: email alsupwebdesign@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Break Glass is a reference. It doesn't replace training, medical care or emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number if you can.

Getting set up

  • Do it while you have signal. Open Break Glass and follow the Get ready list at the top of Guides: your household, a meeting place and contact, the offline library, your state's map and your Medical ID.
  • Downloads are optional. The first-aid cards, the built-in manuals and the tools work without them. The library is about 0.9 GB and a state map 0.2–2 GB, so use Wi-Fi.
  • Language: Settings → Language (English or Español). Manuals and the emergency steps stay in English, the language they were published in.

Common questions

  • Does it work in Airplane Mode? Yes. Leave Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on if you use Talk.
  • Why doesn't Ask write an answer? Ask needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later). Without it, Ask shows the library pages that match your question. Ask's answers come from a small on-device model: check them against the cited pages, and use the amounts and doses in the sources.
  • Talk doesn't find anyone. Talk connects phones running Break Glass that are close by, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. Both people need Talk turned on. Private groups need the same group name and passphrase, or a scanned group code. Talk doesn't contact 911 or any emergency service.
  • Battery saver in Talk: Wi-Fi opens for 3 minutes every quarter hour (:00, :15, :30, :45); Bluetooth stays on. Messages written in between go out at the next window.
  • Outage clock reminders don't appear. Allow notifications for Break Glass in the iPhone's Settings.
  • How do I move my family plan to another phone? Family plan → Share, then AirDrop it. The other phone merges it.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete items in the app, or delete Break Glass to remove everything it stored.

Reporting a message in Talk

Hold a message and tap Report. Break Glass opens an email to us with the message, ready to send the next time you have signal. You can also Block the sender: their messages disappear from your phone.

Privacy
